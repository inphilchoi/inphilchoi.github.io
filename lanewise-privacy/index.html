Lanewise Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · 한국어

This policy applies to both the iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play) versions of Lanewise.

Lanewise does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Your practice history stays on your phone.

1. Data we collect

None. You never sign up or sign in. The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, date of birth or driver's license details. We run no server that receives data from the app.

2. What stays on your device

  • The state and test you chose, and your language setting.
  • Your answers, your review schedule and your mock exam results.
  • Whether Lanewise Pro is owned on this device.

These are saved in the app's own storage on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else. Settings → Reset progress erases them at any time; deleting the app deletes them too. On Android the app does not opt out of system backup, so if you turn on device backup (for example Google account backup) this data may be included in it; that depends on your device and account settings and we have no access to it.

3. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK, does not use the advertising identifier (the Android app does not declare the AD_ID permission) and does not track you across apps or websites.

4. Permissions

Lanewise does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos. It works offline.

The Android app declares only Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING), INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. It requests no contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or storage access.

5. Purchases

Lanewise Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store on iOS and by Google Play Billing on Android. We receive no payment or personal details; the app only checks on the device whether Pro is owned. On Android you can restore the purchase with the same Google account.

6. Links

Links in the app (state driver handbook pages, this policy, and the license agreement: Apple's Standard EULA on iOS, Google Play Terms of Service on Android) open in your browser only when you tap them. Those sites have their own policies.

7. Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

8. Not a government app

Lanewise is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government agency or department of motor vehicles.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.

10. Contact and support

Questions, corrections to a question, or support: email us.
